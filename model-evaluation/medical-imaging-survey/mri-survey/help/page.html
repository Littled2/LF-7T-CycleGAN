Term Definitions

Artefacts

Select the enhancement that introduces fewer artificial structures, ringing, ghosting, or blurring not present in the original low-field image.

Anatomical Structure

Select the enhancement that more accurately preserves the shape, size, and position of anatomical structures such as sulci, ventricles, and grey/white matter boundaries.

Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)

Select the enhancement that appears cleaner and less grainy.

Overall Image Quality

Select the enhancement you would most prefer to use for diagnostic purposes.
